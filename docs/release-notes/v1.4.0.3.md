# MiWRT 1.4.0.3

The router now has a built-in service for the MiWRT phone app, and a new look. Based on Beam WRT 1.4.0.

- **App service.** A small service on the router that the MiWRT iPhone app talks to over the home network. Nothing else is needed and nothing goes through a cloud. The app finds the router by itself and pairs with the router's administrator password.
- **What the app can do with it:** status and live rates; devices with names, categories, icons, traffic and history; pause a device, a person's devices or a room, now or on a schedule; approve new devices (optional); Wi-Fi name, password, on/off and an off-schedule per network; guest Wi-Fi; speed test (download and upload); Wi-Fi channel check; speed shaping (SQM); IoT watch; blocked threats; health history; router lights; Wake-on-LAN; settings backup; firmware update check and install.
- **Ad blocker link.** An AdGuard Home on the network can be linked and managed from the app: on, off, pause, statistics, recent activity, allow or block a site, block lists, and ad blocking off for a single device.
- **Notifications.** Off by default, one switch in the app. The router encrypts each alert's text with a key only the phone has; a relay passes the encrypted notification to Apple without being able to read it. See [Privacy](../../PRIVACY.md).
- **Alerts and recovery.** Radio crashed or recovered, internet or DNS down, new device, radar on 5 GHz. If a Wi-Fi core crashes and stays down for about 90 seconds, the router restarts itself (not in the first 10 minutes after boot, at most three times a day).
- **Separate IoT network helpers.** Where an IoT bridge (`br-iot`) exists, smart-home discovery broadcasts and mDNS (AirPlay, Chromecast, printers) are repeated between it and the main network, so phone apps still find their devices.
- **Look.** The MiWRT mark and indigo accent colours in the web interface.
- **In the image:** curl with HTTP/2.

Install over MiWRT 1.4.0.2 or Beam WRT 1.4.0 with sysupgrade; settings are kept.
